How the funnel works

1. A fresh-looking AfterHour profile posts winning trades, and its bio or link points to a Telegram channel: "free alerts," "VIP signals."
2. The free channel warms you up with signals and profit screenshots — then comes the upsell: VIP access, copy trading, or a "challenge."
3. Deposits go in. Withdrawals never come out. The channel rebrands or the admin ghosts.

Take action

Think you found a scam? Report it:
FTC — ReportFraud.ftc.gov
FBI IC3 — ic3.gov
SEC — sec.gov/tcr

Report a channel to Telegram: every account on this site has a Report to Telegram button next to its listing. It opens your own email app with a pre-filled report to abuse@telegram.org — you send it, the site sends nothing. A copy-the-report-text option sits next to it if your email app won't open from the page. You can also report inside Telegram: open the channel, tap Report, then Scam/Fraud. Reporting helps, but there is no guaranteed threshold — Telegram decides, and a banned channel often reappears under a new name. That is why we keep checking and marking the ones that are gone.
