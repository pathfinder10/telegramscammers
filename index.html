How the funnel works

1. A fresh-looking AfterHour profile posts winning trades, and its bio or link points to a Telegram channel: "free alerts," "VIP signals."
2. The free channel warms you up with signals and profit screenshots — then comes the upsell: VIP access, copy trading, or a "challenge."
3. Deposits go in. Withdrawals never come out. The channel rebrands or the admin ghosts.
