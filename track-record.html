How scoring works

  1. The call. A public Telegram channel posts an option trade with a ticker, strike, expiry, and entry price.
  2. The wait. We wait until the contract expires — no early exits, no cherry-picking.
  3. The grade. Intrinsic value at expiry vs. the channel's quoted entry. Worthless = −100%.

Parsing is automatic and can be wrong; duplicate and cross-posted calls are excluded. The channels' own advertised win claims ("+600%!") are their posts, not verified — and winners dominate what they choose to post. That's the survivorship trick this page exists to counter.

Flagged for investigation — not a finding of fraud. A bad track record doesn't prove anyone committed fraud, and a good one doesn't prove legitimacy.
