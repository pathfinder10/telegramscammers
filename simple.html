How we work

AfterHour profiles post winning trades. Their bio or link points to a Telegram channel — "free alerts," "VIP signals." You join, the signals and profit screenshots warm you up, then comes the upsell: VIP access, copy trading, a "challenge." Deposits go in. Withdrawals never come out.

We graded their trades. We scored 75 of their option calls hold-to-expiry against the entry prices they quoted themselves: 55 expired worthless. They claimed a 93% win rate.

NAME is the AfterHour poster. Under it, Telegram is the channel's display name from its public Telegram preview. Private-invite links (t.me/+…) have no public page, so those show as unknown.

Accounts get in our file when an AfterHour poster links a Telegram channel in their bio or posts. Accounts sharing the same Telegram link are grouped together — that shared link is the coordination fingerprint. A ✓ DELETED mark means the Telegram channel (or the AfterHour account) was checked and found gone on Nov 11. Private-invite links (t.me/+…) have no public page, so they can't be checked for deletion — they're never marked deleted on a guess.

If a listing is wrong, or you have evidence about an account, use the report box above. Listings are flags for investigation, not findings of fraud.
